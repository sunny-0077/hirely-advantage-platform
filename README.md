# Career Catalyst

"Hirely AI" (app name)

An elite, AI-driven career acceleration platform that reverse-engineers the hiring process. It transforms standard job seekers into top-tier candidates by aligning their specific qualifications with live market data, providing dynamic resume architecture, contextual outreach generation, and immersive interview simulations.

The Enhanced Core Features

1. AI-Powered ATS Simulator & Resume Architect (Upgraded from Resume Analyzer)

The Enhancement: Instead of just "scanning" a resume, the app simulates a real enterprise Applicant Tracking System (ATS).

Premium Capabilities: * Generates a definitive "Market Readiness Score" (0-100) based on the target job description.

Provides side-by-side "Impact Rewrites," transforming passive bullet points into data-driven, action-oriented achievements.

Identifies exact missing keywords required to bypass automated HR filters.

2. Contextual Pitch & Outreach Engine (Upgraded from Cover Letter Generator)

The Enhancement: Nobody just sends cover letters anymore. This feature becomes a full suite for professional networking.

Premium Capabilities:

Generates the traditional, highly personalized cover letter.

Creates punchy, 50-word LinkedIn Cold Connection DMs tailored to the hiring manager's profile.

Drafts polite but persistent Follow-Up Emails for post-interview communication.

3. Immersive Behavioral Voice Coach (Upgraded from Interview Prep Simulator)

The Enhancement: Moving from text-based questions to a high-pressure, realistic simulation.

Premium Capabilities:

STAR Method Analyzer: Actively listens to the user's answers to ensure they are using the Situation, Task, Action, Result framework.

Delivery Metrics: Analyzes pacing, use of filler words (um, like), and conversational confidence.

Curveball Generation: Randomly injects high-stress or unexpected questions ("Tell me about a time you failed miserably") to test adaptability.

The "Sticky" Bonus Feature (For User Retention)

★ The Skill Gap Roadmap: Once the user gets the job, they usually leave the app. To keep them coming back, add a feature that analyzes their current resume against the next role they want in 3 years. The AI then generates a personalized, month-by-month roadmap of the exact certifications, skills, or projects they need to secure that future promotion.
